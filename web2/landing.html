The servant’s rules

Three promises. Kept by design.

Not a personality trait — plumbing. You can’t sweet-talk Hearth out of them, and neither can anyone else.

  1. 1

    It asks before it acts.

    Nothing spends, cancels, or unlocks on its own. Every move arrives as a plain-words proposal in your tray and waits for your tap.

    • One-time approvals. Each tap works exactly once — reused approvals are refused.
    • Consequences wait. Money, locks, and limits are always staged — never fired on their own.
  2. 2

    It writes everything down.

    Every proposal, tap, and reversal lands in a record you can read and check — plain words, exact numbers, sealed receipts.

    • Receipts as it happens. Every action is sealed the moment it occurs.
    • Evidence attached. 3 a.m. answers arrive with the reasoning, not just a verdict.
  3. 3

    It undoes anything in one tap.

    Changed your mind — or the house did? Undo runs the exact reverse: the door re-locks, the thermostat is restored, the cart is withdrawn, the money comes back.

    • Reversible by construction. Every action carries its own reverse — built in, not patched on.
    • Kids are walled off, not talked to. Child requests are refused before a plan even exists.

Questions

Asked by actual families

Will Hearth ever spend my money without asking?

No. Anything consequential — spending, cancelling, unlocking — is staged in the approval tray and waits for your tap. Each approval is single-use, and every approval can be undone in one tap.

Do I need an Amazon device to use the demo?

No. The demo runs in any browser and is designed for the Echo Show’s screen. In this preview, your household is a simulated twin — no real accounts or devices are touched.

What if I approve something by mistake?

Tap Undo. Hearth keeps a compensating reverse for every action — the door re-locks, the thermostat is restored, the cart is withdrawn — and records the reversal in its tamper-evident ledger.

Can my kids order things or unlock doors?

No. Child profiles are refused before any plan is drafted. It’s architectural, not a prompt — which is why “pretend you’re Santa” doesn’t work.

Is my data safe?

The preview runs locally on your machine. The code is open source under MIT — you can audit exactly what Hearth reads, stages, and stores.

Is this really built for Alexa+?

Yes — Hearth is a self-hosted MCP server speaking the open standard the Alexa+ ecosystem builds on (spec 2025-11-25, over Streamable HTTP), plus this simulated Alexa+ experience you can drive right here in the browser.
