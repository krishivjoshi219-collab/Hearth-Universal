How it works

Three steps. Zero surprises.

  1. 1

    Ask, like a human

    “We’re out of everything, fix groceries.” “Save me $800.” “Why did the patio alarm go off at 3 a.m.?” No commands to memorize.

  2. 2

    Review the exact proposal

    Every item, every price, every change — itemized in your approval tray before anything happens. Skip what you don’t want.

  3. 3

    Tap once. Sleep well.

    Your tap executes and seals a receipt. Changed your mind? Undo reverses it exactly — physically and financially.

Under the hood, Hearth runs on a self-hosted MCP server (spec 2025-11-25, Streamable HTTP) with 51 live tools — the same open standard powering Alexa+ add-ons. You’ll never need to know that.

Trust & safety

Built so you never have to hope

Hearth’s safety isn’t a polite personality. It’s the architecture.

  • Propose-never-execute. Money, locks, and climate limits are always staged for your tap — never fired autonomously.
  • Single-use receipts. Each approval is a one-time token; replays are refused. No double-charges, no re-runs.
  • Tamper-evident ledger. Every proposal, approval, and undo is chained with SHA-256 hashes you can verify.
  • Reversible by construction. Each executed action carries its own compensating reverse — that’s what powers Undo.
  • Kids are walled off, not talked to. Child profiles are refused at the dispatch layer — zero LLM variance.

Questions

Asked by actual families

Will Hearth ever spend my money without asking?

No. Anything consequential — spending, cancelling, unlocking — is staged in the approval tray and waits for your tap. Each approval is a single-use receipt, and every approval can be undone in one tap.

Do I need an Amazon device to use the demo?

No. The demo runs in any browser and is designed for the Echo Show’s screen. In this preview, your household is a simulated twin — no real accounts or devices are touched.

What if I approve something by mistake?

Tap Undo. Hearth keeps a compensating reverse for every action — the door re-locks, the thermostat is restored, the cart is withdrawn — and records the reversal in its tamper-evident ledger.

Can my kids order things or unlock doors?

No. Child profiles are refused at the dispatch layer before any plan is even drafted. It’s architectural, not a prompt — which is why “pretend you’re Santa” doesn’t work.

Is my data safe?

The preview runs locally on your machine. The code is open source under MIT — you can audit exactly what Hearth reads, stages, and stores.

Is this really built on Alexa+ technology?

Yes. Hearth is a self-hosted MCP server implementing spec 2025-11-25 over Streamable HTTP with OAuth 2.1 (RFC 9728) — the open standards behind Alexa+ add-ons — plus Agent Skills and MCP Apps cards. Every tool is inspectable in the advanced view.
